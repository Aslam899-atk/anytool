Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Zero File Retention Policy

At AnyTool.io, your privacy is our foundational architectural principle. The overwhelming majority of our tools (including Background Remover, Image Converters, QR Scanner, Hash Generators, AES Encryption, and Code Formatters) operate 100% locally inside your browser memory using WebAssembly and client-side Web APIs.

2. Memory-Only Processing for Server Tools

For operations that require backend assistance (such as PDF Merging via Express), files are processed strictly in RAM (volatile memory) via Multer memory storage and immediately discarded after delivery. We never write your uploaded documents or images to server hard disks.

3. No Accounts, Cookies, or Tracking

AnyTool.io does not require user accounts, passwords, or emails. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or sell user analytics to advertising brokers.

4. Local Storage

Preferences such as your chosen theme (dark/light), selected language (English/Malayalam), and favorite tools are stored locally on your device via localStorage and never sent to our servers.
