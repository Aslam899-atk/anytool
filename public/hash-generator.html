Why Use Cryptographic Hashes in Cyber Security?

🛡️

File Integrity Verification

Software vendors publish SHA-256 checksums so downloaders can verify that the downloaded installer was not infected with trojans.

🔒

One-Way Hashing

A cryptographic hash cannot be reversed back to original plaintext. Even a single changed character completely alters the hash (Avalanche effect).
